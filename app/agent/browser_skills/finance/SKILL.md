# Finance Skill

## FOR STOCK RESEARCH: Navigate to https://finance.yahoo.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to Yahoo Finance
2. Enter ticker symbol or company name in search bar
3. Extract: current price, change, market cap, P/E ratio, dividend yield
4. Check "Summary", "Statistics", and "Financials" tabs for details
5. Present key metrics in a structured format

## FOR PRODUCT COMPARISON: Navigate to https://www.nerdwallet.com
- Credit cards: search by category (travel, cashback, balance transfer)
- Savings accounts: compare APY rates
- Loans: compare rates and terms

## FOR INSURANCE: Navigate to comparison sites
- Auto insurance: the-zebra.com
- Home insurance: policygenius.com

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Stock prices are delayed 15-20 minutes on free sites
- "Sponsored" financial products appear first on comparison sites
- APR vs APY — make sure to specify which is shown
- Credit card offers vary by creditworthiness — note the range
