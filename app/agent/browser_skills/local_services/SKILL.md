# Local Services Skill

## SITE: Navigate to https://www.google.com/maps or https://www.yelp.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Search Google Maps for "[service] near [location]"
2. Browse results — note: business name, rating, review count, hours, distance
3. Check top 3 results for: reviews, price range, services offered
4. If more detail needed, click on the business for phone number, website, hours
5. Present top 3-5 options with ratings and key info

## ALTERNATIVES:
- Yelp (yelp.com) — detailed reviews, photos
- Thumbtack (thumbtack.com) — get quotes from providers
- Angi (angi.com) — home services specifically

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Google Maps results are influenced by ads — "Sponsored" results appear first
- Check review count, not just rating (5 stars with 2 reviews < 4.5 stars with 200 reviews)
- "Open now" filter is useful for urgent needs
- Some businesses have outdated hours or closed permanently
