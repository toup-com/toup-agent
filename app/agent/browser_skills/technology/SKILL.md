# Technology Research Skill

## FOR SOFTWARE COMPARISON: Navigate to https://www.g2.com or https://www.capterra.com

## FOR TECH REVIEWS: Navigate to theverge.com, cnet.com, or pcmag.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to appropriate review/comparison site
2. Search for the product or category
3. Extract: product name, rating, pricing, key features, pros/cons
4. For comparisons, create a side-by-side feature table
5. Present findings with clear recommendation

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- G2/Capterra reviews may be incentivized — check review text quality
- Tech specs change frequently — verify current pricing on product website
- "Editor's Choice" badges may be paid placements
