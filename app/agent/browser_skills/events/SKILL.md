# Event Tickets Skill

## SITE: Navigate to https://www.ticketmaster.com or https://www.stubhub.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to Ticketmaster (primary market) or StubHub (resale)
2. Search for the event (artist, team, show name)
3. Filter by: date, venue, city
4. Browse available events — note: date, venue, price range
5. Click on an event for seat availability
6. Extract: event name, date, venue, price range, seat sections available
7. Present options with price ranges

## ALTERNATIVE: SeatGeek (seatgeek.com)
- Good for price comparison across primary and resale markets
- Shows a "Deal Score" rating for each ticket

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Ticketmaster has aggressive bot detection — may show CAPTCHAs
- Prices shown may not include fees (Ticketmaster adds ~20-30% in fees)
- "Verified Resale" tickets on Ticketmaster are from resellers (higher prices)
- Popular events may have no tickets available — check StubHub resale
