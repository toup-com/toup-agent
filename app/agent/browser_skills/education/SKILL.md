# Education Skill

## SITE: Depends on what user wants
- Online courses: https://www.coursera.org or https://www.udemy.com
- Free learning: https://www.khanacademy.org
- University info: Google search → specific university site
- Language learning: https://www.duolingo.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to the appropriate learning platform
2. Search for the course/topic
3. Apply filters: free/paid, difficulty level, rating, duration
4. Browse results — extract: course name, instructor, rating, duration, price
5. Click on top results for syllabus and reviews
6. Present top 3-5 options

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Coursera/edX: distinguish between free audit and paid certificate
- Udemy: prices are heavily discounted during sales — check if a sale is active
- Course ratings can be inflated — check review text, not just stars
