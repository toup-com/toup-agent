# Legal Skill

## FOR FINDING LAWYERS: Navigate to https://www.avvo.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to Avvo or FindLaw
2. Search by practice area and location
3. Browse results — note: rating, reviews, experience, practice areas
4. Present top options with credentials

## FOR LEGAL INFO: Google Scholar (scholar.google.com) has free case law
## FOR DOCUMENT TEMPLATES: LegalZoom (legalzoom.com) has common templates

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Do NOT provide legal advice — only present factual information from websites
- Legal information varies by jurisdiction — always note the state/country
- Paywalled databases (Westlaw, LexisNexis) may not be accessible
