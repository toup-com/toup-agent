# Recipe Search Skill

## SITE: Navigate to https://www.allrecipes.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to AllRecipes (or Google search "recipe for [dish]")
2. Search for the recipe by name
3. Click on the best matching result (highest rated, most reviews)
4. SCROLL past the story/introduction to find the actual recipe
5. Extract: ingredients list, step-by-step instructions, prep time, cook time, servings
6. Present in a clean, structured format

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Recipe sites have LONG introductions before the recipe — scroll down to "Ingredients"
- Look for the "Jump to Recipe" button at the top to skip the story
- Servings may need scaling — note the default serving size
- Some sites show recipe in a card format at the bottom, not inline
