# Restaurant Reservation Skill

## SITE: Navigate to https://www.opentable.com or https://resy.com

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to OpenTable or Resy
2. Enter location/restaurant name in search
3. Set date, time, and party size
4. Browse available restaurants
5. Click on a restaurant to see details (cuisine, rating, price range)
6. Look for available time slots
7. Present options: restaurant name, cuisine, rating, price range, available times

## ALTERNATIVE: Google Maps
- Search "restaurants near [location]" on Google Maps
- Filter by cuisine, price, rating, hours
- Check if reservations are available through Google

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Popular restaurants may have no availability — suggest alternatives
- Time zones matter — confirm the restaurant's local time
- Some restaurants only take reservations via phone or their own website
