# News Skill

## SITE: Navigate to https://news.google.com or a specific news site

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to Google News or the user's preferred news source
2. Search for the topic
3. Read headlines and snippets from multiple sources
4. Click on the most relevant article
5. Extract: headline, source, date, key facts, quotes
6. If the user wants multiple perspectives, read 2-3 articles from different sources
7. Present a summary with key facts and source attribution

## FOR BREAKING NEWS:
- Reuters (reuters.com) and AP News (apnews.com) are fastest
- CNN, BBC for live coverage

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Paywalls: NYT, WSJ, WaPo limit free articles — try Google News links or Reuters/AP instead
- Cookie consent banners (especially EU sites) — dismiss them
- Auto-playing video may overlay the article text
- Check the article date — old articles may appear in results
