# Government Services Skill

## KEY SITES:
- Federal: usa.gov, irs.gov, uscis.gov, ssa.gov
- State: varies by state (e.g., dmv.ca.gov)

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to the appropriate government website
2. Find the relevant service or form
3. Read instructions carefully before filling forms
4. Fill forms step by step — government forms often have strict validation
5. Note any required documents or fees

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- Government sites often have session timeouts — work quickly
- Forms may use outdated UI (no autocomplete, no autosave)
- Some sites only work in specific browsers or have Java/Flash requirements
- File upload limits may be small (1-5MB)
- Double-check all entries — government form corrections are difficult
