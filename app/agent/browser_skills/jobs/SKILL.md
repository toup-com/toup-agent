# Job Search Skill

## SITE: Navigate to https://www.indeed.com or https://www.linkedin.com/jobs

## PROCEDURE:
1. Navigate to Indeed or LinkedIn Jobs
2. Enter job title/keywords in the "What" field
3. Enter location in the "Where" field
4. Click Search
5. Apply filters: salary range, experience level, remote/hybrid/onsite, date posted
6. Browse listings — extract: title, company, location, salary, date posted
7. Click on promising listings for full details
8. Present top 5-10 matching jobs with key details

## SALARY RESEARCH:
- Use glassdoor.com for salary data and company reviews
- Use levels.fyi for tech company compensation data

## COMMON PITFALLS:
- LinkedIn requires login for most features — Indeed doesn't
- "Easy Apply" jobs on LinkedIn require a profile
- Salary ranges are often missing — check Glassdoor for estimates
- "Sponsored" listings appear first and may not be the best match
- Some listings are from staffing agencies, not the actual employer
